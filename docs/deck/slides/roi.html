The project’s own definition of return: flags found per process review. In testing that is the share of deliberately planted defects that were flagged: 36 of 36 across three repeats of 12 defect cases. Clean cases in the same test set must also raise no major or critical flag, which guards against false alarms. What the repository does not have: how many flags supervisors catch today, how many reviews per week, what a review costs now. Those are needed before any dollar figure. The false-flag rate needs one new thing: a place where the reviewer marks each flag right or wrong. Today a sign-off records only approve or deny.
