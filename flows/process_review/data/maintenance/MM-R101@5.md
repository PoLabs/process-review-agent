## Description

Manual: jacketed reactor R-101. This manual is kept by the maintenance team for the operators of R-101.

## Ratings

R-101 is rated for a maximum pressure of 6 bar and a maximum temperature of 150 °C. Pressure above the rating is a critical event and the vessel is stopped at once.

## Status

R-101 is in service.

## Maintenance

Inspect gaskets and seals every quarter and record the result in the equipment log. Report any leak or abnormal noise to maintenance before the next use.
