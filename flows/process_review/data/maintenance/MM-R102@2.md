## Description

Manual: small reactor R-102. This manual is kept by the maintenance team for the operators of R-102.

## Ratings

R-102 is rated for a maximum pressure of 5 bar and a maximum temperature of 120 °C. Pressure above the rating is a critical event.

## Status

R-102 is in service.

## Maintenance

Inspect gaskets and seals every quarter and record the result in the equipment log. Report any leak or abnormal noise to maintenance before the next use.
