## Description

Manual: reactor R-2 (retired). This manual is kept by the maintenance team for the operators of R-2.

## Ratings

R-2 was rated for a maximum pressure of 2 bar. The reactor is retired and this manual is kept for the record only.

## Status

R-2 is not in service and must not be used.

## Maintenance

Inspect gaskets and seals every quarter and record the result in the equipment log. Report any leak or abnormal noise to maintenance before the next use.
