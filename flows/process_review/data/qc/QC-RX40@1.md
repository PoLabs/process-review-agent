## Product

Specification: RX-40 resin. This specification applies to every batch of RX-40 made on the floor.

## Composition

RX-40 is made from acrylic resin AR7 blended with toluene.

## Cure

Cure at 135 °C ± 5 °C for 60 minutes. The cure step needs equipment rated for at least 140 °C.

## Release

A batch is released only after the supervisor confirms that the recorded values match this specification. Out-of-range values are reported to quality before the batch is moved.
