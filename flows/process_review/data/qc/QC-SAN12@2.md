## Product

Specification: SAN-12 sanitizer. This specification applies to every batch of SAN-12 made on the floor.

## Composition

SAN-12 contains sodium hypochlorite at 5.0 to 6.0 % in water. A value outside this range makes the batch non-conforming.

## Process

Mix at 15 to 25 °C in the open tank M-7. Do not heat the batch.

## Release

A batch is released only after the supervisor confirms that the recorded values match this specification. Out-of-range values are reported to quality before the batch is moved.
