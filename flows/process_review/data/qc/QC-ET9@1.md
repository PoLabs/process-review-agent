## Product

Specification: ET-9 etchant. This specification applies to every batch of ET-9 made on the floor.

## Process

ET-9 is an etchant. Blend at 15 to 25 °C.

## Composition

The acid content is set by the formulation card issued with each work order.

## Release

A batch is released only after the supervisor confirms that the recorded values match this specification. Out-of-range values are reported to quality before the batch is moved.
