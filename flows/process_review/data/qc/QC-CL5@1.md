## Product

Specification: CL-5 degreaser. This specification applies to every batch of CL-5 made on the floor.

## Process

CL-5 is a solvent degreaser. Blend at ambient temperature, 15 to 30 °C.

## Composition

The solvent blend is set by the formulation card; no oxidizer is part of the recipe.

## Release

A batch is released only after the supervisor confirms that the recorded values match this specification. Out-of-range values are reported to quality before the batch is moved.
