## Product

Specification: CX-200 clear coat. This specification applies to every batch of CX-200 made on the floor.

## Process

CX-200 is made in reactor R-101. Mix at 40 to 50 °C until clear, which normally takes 90 minutes.

## Composition

CX-200 is a toluene-based coating containing acrylic resin AR7. No other solvent is part of the recipe.

## Release

A batch is released only after the supervisor confirms that the recorded values match this specification. Out-of-range values are reported to quality before the batch is moved.
