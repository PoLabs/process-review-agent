## Product

Specification: NX-1 neutralizer. This specification applies to every batch of NX-1 made on the floor.

## Composition

NX-1 contains sodium hydroxide at 9 to 11 % in water.

## Process

Blend at 20 to 35 °C. Add the sodium hydroxide slowly to the water.

## Release

A batch is released only after the supervisor confirms that the recorded values match this specification. Out-of-range values are reported to quality before the batch is moved.
