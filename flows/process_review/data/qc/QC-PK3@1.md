## Product

Specification: PK-3 pickling solution. This specification applies to every batch of PK-3 made on the floor.

## Composition

PK-3 contains hydrochloric acid at 9 to 11 % in water.

## Process

Blend at 15 to 30 °C. Add the acid slowly to the water.

## Release

A batch is released only after the supervisor confirms that the recorded values match this specification. Out-of-range values are reported to quality before the batch is moved.
