## Identification

This safety data sheet covers nitric acid as received from the supplier. It is issued for use by floor supervisors and operators at the plant.

## Hazards

Nitric acid is an oxidizing acid. It causes severe burns and its fumes are toxic when inhaled. It can ignite organic material on contact.

## Handling and storage

Store in the acid cabinet in the original container, separate from organics. Dilute only by adding acid slowly to water. Wear face shield, gloves and apron.

## Incompatible materials

Nitric acid reacts violently with alcohols and ketones. Keep it away from solvents, bases and metals.

## First aid

Move the person to fresh air and remove contaminated clothing. Rinse skin or eyes with water for at least 15 minutes and call the site first-aider. Report every exposure to the shift supervisor.
