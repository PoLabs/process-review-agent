## Identification

This safety data sheet covers acetone as received from the supplier. It is issued for use by floor supervisors and operators at the plant.

## Hazards

Acetone is a highly flammable liquid with a flash point of -20 °C. Vapours can travel to an ignition source and flash back. Prolonged contact dries the skin and the vapour irritates the eyes.

## Handling and storage

Keep containers closed and grounded when transferring. Store in the flammables cabinet away from heat and sparks. Use only in areas with ventilation and no open flames.

## Incompatible materials

Keep acetone away from oxidizers, in particular hydrogen peroxide and nitric acid. Contact can cause fire or violent reaction.

## First aid

Move the person to fresh air and remove contaminated clothing. Rinse skin or eyes with water for at least 15 minutes and call the site first-aider. Report every exposure to the shift supervisor.
