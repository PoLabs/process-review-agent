## Identification

This safety data sheet covers hydrogen peroxide as received from the supplier. It is issued for use by floor supervisors and operators at the plant.

## Hazards

Hydrogen peroxide is a strong oxidizer. It can ignite combustible material and decomposes releasing oxygen. Concentrated solutions burn skin and eyes.

## Handling and storage

Store in the vented original container in a cool, shaded place. Never return unused product to the container. Use only clean, compatible equipment.

## Incompatible materials

Never mix hydrogen peroxide with ketones or alcohols. Avoid metals, organic solvents and any combustible material.

## First aid

Move the person to fresh air and remove contaminated clothing. Rinse skin or eyes with water for at least 15 minutes and call the site first-aider. Report every exposure to the shift supervisor.
