## Identification

This safety data sheet covers hydrochloric acid as received from the supplier. It is issued for use by floor supervisors and operators at the plant.

## Hazards

Hydrochloric acid is corrosive. It releases hydrogen chloride fumes that damage the airways, eyes and equipment.

## Handling and storage

Handle only where fume extraction is running. Store in the acid cabinet. Add acid slowly to water, never the reverse.

## Incompatible materials

Keep hydrochloric acid away from hypochlorite bleach, strong bases and metals.

## First aid

Move the person to fresh air and remove contaminated clothing. Rinse skin or eyes with water for at least 15 minutes and call the site first-aider. Report every exposure to the shift supervisor.
