## Identification

This safety data sheet covers ammonia as received from the supplier. It is issued for use by floor supervisors and operators at the plant.

## Hazards

Aqueous ammonia is an irritant to eyes, skin and the airways. The vapour has a sharp smell and is harmful in enclosed spaces.

## Handling and storage

Store in closed containers in a ventilated area. Open containers only where there is local exhaust.

## Incompatible materials

Do not mix ammonia with bleach or other hypochlorite products, or with strong acids.

## First aid

Move the person to fresh air and remove contaminated clothing. Rinse skin or eyes with water for at least 15 minutes and call the site first-aider. Report every exposure to the shift supervisor.
