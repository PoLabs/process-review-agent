## Identification

This safety data sheet covers sodium hypochlorite as received from the supplier. It is issued for use by floor supervisors and operators at the plant.

## Hazards

Sodium hypochlorite solution is corrosive and irritating to skin and eyes. Contact with acids or ammonia releases toxic gas. It slowly decomposes and loses strength in storage.

## Handling and storage

Store in the original vented container, out of sunlight. Use only dedicated, clean transfer equipment. Never decant into a container that held acid.

## Incompatible materials

Never mix sodium hypochlorite with ammonia or acids. Toxic gas (chlorine or chloramine) is released.

## First aid

Move the person to fresh air and remove contaminated clothing. Rinse skin or eyes with water for at least 15 minutes and call the site first-aider. Report every exposure to the shift supervisor.
