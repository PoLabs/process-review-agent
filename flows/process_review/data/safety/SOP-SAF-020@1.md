## Scope

This procedure covers the use of hydrochloric acid in blending and pickling processes. It applies to every operator and supervisor who handles the acid.

## Required controls

Hydrochloric acid may only be used while the fume scrubber SC-1 is in service. If SC-1 is locked out or under repair, no process with hydrochloric acid is started.

## Personal protection

Wear face shield, acid-resistant gloves, apron and boots. Keep an eyewash station within reach of the work area.
