## Identification

This safety data sheet covers toluene as received from the supplier. It is issued for use by floor supervisors and operators at the plant.

## Hazards

Toluene is a flammable liquid with a flash point of 4 °C. The vapour causes drowsiness and headache and is harmful on repeated exposure.

## Handling and storage

Ground and bond containers during transfer. Store in the flammables cabinet and keep closed. Provide local exhaust at open transfer points.

## Incompatible materials

Keep toluene away from strong oxidizers and strong acids.

## First aid

Move the person to fresh air and remove contaminated clothing. Rinse skin or eyes with water for at least 15 minutes and call the site first-aider. Report every exposure to the shift supervisor.
