## Identification

This safety data sheet covers acrylic resin ar7 as received from the supplier. It is issued for use by floor supervisors and operators at the plant.

## Hazards

Acrylic resin AR7 is a low hazard resin solution. It can cause mild skin irritation on repeated contact.

## Handling and storage

Store in closed containers at ordinary indoor conditions. Wipe up spills and wash hands after use.

## Incompatible materials

No special incompatibilities are known beyond strong oxidizers.

## First aid

Move the person to fresh air and remove contaminated clothing. Rinse skin or eyes with water for at least 15 minutes and call the site first-aider. Report every exposure to the shift supervisor.
