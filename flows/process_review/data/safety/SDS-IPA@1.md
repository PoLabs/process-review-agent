## Identification

This safety data sheet covers isopropanol as received from the supplier. It is issued for use by floor supervisors and operators at the plant.

## Hazards

Isopropanol is a flammable liquid with a flash point of 12 °C. The vapour irritates the eyes and can cause drowsiness.

## Handling and storage

Store in the flammables cabinet in closed containers. Ground containers when transferring in bulk. Keep away from ignition sources.

## Incompatible materials

Keep isopropanol away from strong oxidizers and nitric acid.

## First aid

Move the person to fresh air and remove contaminated clothing. Rinse skin or eyes with water for at least 15 minutes and call the site first-aider. Report every exposure to the shift supervisor.
