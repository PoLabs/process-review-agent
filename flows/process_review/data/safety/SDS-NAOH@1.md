## Identification

This safety data sheet covers sodium hydroxide as received from the supplier. It is issued for use by floor supervisors and operators at the plant.

## Hazards

Sodium hydroxide solution is corrosive. It causes severe burns to skin and eyes and damages some metals such as aluminium.

## Handling and storage

Store in closed plastic or steel containers. Add sodium hydroxide slowly to water while stirring. Wear face shield, gloves and apron.

## Incompatible materials

Keep sodium hydroxide away from strong acids and from aluminium or zinc equipment.

## First aid

Move the person to fresh air and remove contaminated clothing. Rinse skin or eyes with water for at least 15 minutes and call the site first-aider. Report every exposure to the shift supervisor.
